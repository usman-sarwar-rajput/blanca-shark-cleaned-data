# Provenance and reuse

## Original observations

The juvenile white shark telemetry was collected by the Monterey Bay Aquarium collaboration and archived at https://doi.org/10.24431/rw1k6c3. Its data-description article is https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01235-3. The upstream dataset is described as CC BY; preserve attribution and check its exact version and licence statement when reusing the source records.

## This release

Usman Sarwar Rajput prepared this derived, Blanca-specific analysis release from existing records. The folders contain processed hourly shark measures, modelled environmental joins, five-stage descriptive summaries, alternate comparisons, diagnostics, and scripts. `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` records the upstream local source filenames and checksums. Original manufacturer tag exports and downloaded weather source files are not republished here.

The main paper values are in `five_stage_comparison/five_stage_summary.csv`. They are descriptive summaries for one shark. The cyclone's calendar dates and modelled distance do not by themselves establish severe local exposure or predictive performance. Consult `ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md`, the comparison README, and validation outputs before reuse.

No blanket licence is asserted over third-party observations, weather products, or code whose ownership has not been established. Cite the upstream dataset and this derived release separately.
