# Three-day descriptive comparison

Additional exploratory visualization. No new significance tests were run. The original analysis remains unchanged.

Reference: 8 June 2015 at 12:00 UTC. Each daily interval starts and ends at 12:00 UTC, rather than midnight. Baseline: 25 May at 12:00 through 5 June at 12:00. These comparison days are not certified storm-free.

| Period | Depth (m) | Vertical movement proxy (m/min) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Baseline (11 days) | 3.60 | 0.278 |
| 3rd day before | 3.67 | 0.391 |
| 2nd day before | 2.99 | 0.374 |
| Last day before | 3.84 | 0.562 |
| 1st day after | 4.48 | 0.326 |
| 2nd day after | 4.11 | 0.295 |
| 3rd day after | 4.46 | 0.377 |

Depth is the average of hourly median depths; the movement proxy is the hourly average of absolute changes between adjacent one-minute median depths. Taller depth bars mean deeper water, unlike the inverted depth axis in the earlier time-series chart. Dashed horizontal lines mark baseline averages.

The movement increase is concentrated in the final day before the reference. A wider view describes persistence and recovery but does not determine whether the storm caused the change or whether it began before local environmental cues. Severe local exposure remains unverified; the existing sensitivity and multiple-testing limitations still apply.

Weather panel: the abrupt wave-model change at 9 June 21:00 is a previously flagged source discontinuity. It falls inside this extended after-period, so interpreting the extended wave comparison requires particular caution. Source data have not been corrected or replaced.

Files: daily_comparison.csv gives exact interval boundaries; combined_comparison.csv gives the 72-hour summaries. Both charts are exported as PNG and vector PDF. All hourly data and models are inherited from the completed analysis.
